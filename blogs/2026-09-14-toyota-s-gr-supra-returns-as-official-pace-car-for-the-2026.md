PLANO, Texas (Sept. 14, 2026) — The GR Supra will once again serve as the Official Pace Car for the National Auto Sport Association (NASA) Championships, returning to lead the field at one of America's most iconic road racing venues. The 20th annual NASA Championships will take place September 24–27, 2026, at Sebring International Raceway in Sebring, Florida.
