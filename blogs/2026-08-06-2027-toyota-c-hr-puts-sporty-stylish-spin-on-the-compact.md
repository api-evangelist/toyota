PLANO, Texas (Aug. 6, 2026) - The reinvented 2027 Toyota C-HR is charged up for the second year in its Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV) form. With standard electronic all-wheel drive, 338 total system horsepower and a manufacturer-estimated 4.9-second 0-60 mph time, it's ready to perform as a daily runabout.
