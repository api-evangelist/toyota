PLANO, Texas (Sept. 9, 2026) – Music has the power to inspire creativity, build confidence and strengthen communities. Through its continued partnership with Music Will, the nation's largest nonprofit music education program, Toyota is helping bring those opportunities to more students through a $75,000 donation and a series of unforgettable real-world experiences that connect them beyond the classroom.
