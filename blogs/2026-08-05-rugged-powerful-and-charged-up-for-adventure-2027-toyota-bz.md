PLANO, Texas (Aug. 5, 2026) – The bZ Woodland, Toyota’s rugged addition to its battery-electric (BEV) lineup, is back for its second year and is expected to arrive at dealerships nationwide later this year. Blending midsize SUV utility with exhilarating electric performance, it’s equipped with standard all-wheel drive (AWD) featuring X-MODE® and brings a highly capable, adventure-ready twist to the bZ family.
