PLANO, Texas (Sept. 8, 2026) — Toyota has donated the Toyota GR Supra MkV Final Edition to Reach Out WorldWide (ROWW), a historic gift that will help support the organization’s disaster relief mission. The one-of-one vehicle is the very last MkV GR Supra produced, a highly collectible, 382-horsepower, six-speed manual Supra featuring commemorative details and factory-exclusive specifications, underscoring Toyota’s commitment to honoring the model’s legacy while supporting a meaningful cause.
